# Quickstart Guide

Learn how to connect your wallet, issue your first receipt, and verify it on ArcScan.

## Steps

1. Connect your Web3 Wallet (MetaMask / Arc compatible).
2. Create a payment request or merchant QR code.
3. Complete settlement in USDC.
4. View your digital receipt and ArcScan verification hash.
