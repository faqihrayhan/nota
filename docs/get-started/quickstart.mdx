# Quickstart Guide

Welcome to **Nota on Arc**. This guide provides a step-by-step walkthrough for merchants and users to set up their EVM wallet, issue instant USDC payments, generate verifiable receipts, manage split bills, and analyze financial cashflow.

---

## Prerequisites

Before using Nota, ensure you have:
1. **An EVM-Compatible Wallet**: MetaMask, Rabby, or WalletConnect-enabled wallet.
2. **Arc Network RPC Configured**: Arc Testnet / Mainnet RPC added to your wallet.
3. **Testnet USDC / Arc Gas Tokens**: Small amount of native Arc tokens for gas fees and USDC for settlements.

---

## Step-by-Step Feature Walkthrough

### 1. Connect Wallet & Authenticate (Auth & RLS)
1. Navigate to [Nota App](https://nota-pay.vercel.app).
2. Click **Connect Wallet** in the top right corner.
3. Approve the cryptographic sign-in request in your wallet.
   - *Nota generates a short-lived, per-wallet Supabase JWT to isolate your merchant catalog and transaction history via Row-Level Security (RLS).*

---

### 2. Merchant Storefront & Catalog Setup (`/merchant`)
1. Open the **Merchant** menu from the navigation bar.
2. **Add Products**: Click `+ Add Item` to create items in your catalog (Title, Description, Price in USDC/IDR/SGD/MYR).
3. **Generate Static/Dynamic QR Code**:
   - Select catalog items to generate a dynamic merchant payment QR code.
   - Or click **Display Merchant QR** for a fixed store payment point.
4. **Live Incoming Order Monitor**: Real-time polling tracks incoming customer payments and auto-verifies on-chain settlement every 2 seconds.

---

### 3. Processing Payments (`/payment`)
1. Go to the **Payment** section.
2. Enter the recipient merchant wallet address or scan a **Nota Payment QR Code**.
3. Choose your preferred display currency (USDC, IDR, MYR, SGD) — values dynamically convert using real-time price feeds while settling on-chain in USDC.
4. Click **Pay Now** and confirm the transaction in MetaMask.
5. Once confirmed, Nota detects the transaction receipt (`txReceipt`) on Arc Network and updates the status to **SETTLED**.

---

### 4. Digital Receipt Export & ArcScan Verification
1. After settlement, a **Digital Receipt Modal** pops up displaying:
   - Unique Cryptographic `dataHash`.
   - On-chain Transaction Hash (`txHash`).
   - Line items, timestamp, and block number.
2. **Export Options**: Click **Download PDF** or **Save PNG** to store an offline copy of your receipt.
3. **Verify on ArcScan**: Click **Verify on ArcScan** to inspect the `ReceiptAnchored` event log on the public blockchain explorer.

---

### 5. Split Bill Payments (`/split-bill`)

Split bills are settled fully on-chain through the `NotaInvoiceManager` contract. The host opens a split, each participant pays their share, and the host closes it out.

1. Navigate to **Split Bill**.
2. Enter the total bill amount and add each participant with their share in USDC.
3. Click **Create Split**. Your wallet signs two transactions:
   - `approve` on USDC for the total amount (skipped if the allowance is already sufficient).
   - `createSplit(totalAmount, participants, shares, dataHash)` on `NotaInvoiceManager`. Amounts are sent in micro-USDC (6 decimals). The `dataHash` is a `keccak256` of the canonical split payload (type, nonce, host, participant names, total) and anchors the off-chain metadata to the on-chain record.
4. Once the transaction is mined, Nota reads the new `splitId` from `splitCount()` and generates a QR / link that embeds it.
5. Share the QR with your group. When a participant scans and pays, their wallet calls `paySplit(splitId)`, which transfers their share of USDC directly to the host and emits `SplitMemberPaid`.
6. After every participant has paid, the host clicks **Complete Split** to call `completeSplit(splitId)`. The split is marked settled and can no longer accept payments.

Each step emits an event you can verify on ArcScan: `SplitCreated` for the host's transaction and `SplitMemberPaid` for each participant. See [Split Bill Contract Methods](/docs/smart-contracts/split-bill) for the full method signatures.

---

### 6. Cashflow Analytics & AI Insights (`/analisa` & `/forecast`)

1. Open **Analisa** to view your financial dashboard:
   - **Inflow vs Outflow totals**: Total USDC received into your wallet versus total USDC spent from it, plus net balance for the selected period.
   - **Cashflow trend**: Daily inflow (green) versus outflow (amber).
   - **Settlement velocity**: Average time customer payments take to clear on Arc Network.

2. **Scope toggle (Inflow / Outflow / All).** Inside the **By Category** card, switch the scope to filter both the category breakdown and the recent-transactions list:
   - **Inflow** — only transactions where you are the payee.
   - **Outflow** — only transactions where you are the payer.
   - **All** — both sides combined.

   Recent transactions render with ArcScan-style signed amounts: `+` in green for inflow, `−` in amber for outflow.

3. **Four feature-based categories.** Analisa groups transactions by the Nota feature that produced them, not by shopping tags. The four categories are:
   - **Payment** — direct or generic Scan & Pay payments you sent.
   - **Merchant POS** — payments settled through a merchant storefront QR (with catalog items).
   - **Split Bill** — payments settled through a Split Bill QR or link.
   - **Receive** — incoming transfers to your wallet that aren't tagged as POS or Split Bill.

   Legacy shopping-style tags (for example `belanja`, `makan`, `transport`) are mapped into these four buckets so historical rows still show a consistent label.

4. **How each payment is classified.** The category shown depends on which side of the transaction you're on, so the payer and payee never double-count the same payment:
   - A **Merchant POS** payment (Scan & Pay against a merchant QR with items) shows as **Payment** in your outflow when you're the buyer, and as **Merchant POS** in the merchant's inflow.
   - A **Split Bill** payment shows as **Split Bill** for both the payer and the payee.
   - A **generic Scan & Pay** transfer (no catalog items) shows as **Payment** for the payer and **Receive** for the payee.

5. **Exports.** Click **Export CSV** or **Export PDF** to download the current view. Rows use the same feature category labels (Payment, Merchant POS, Split Bill, Receive) as the dashboard, so exports match what you see in Analisa.

6. Open **Forecast** for AI-driven revenue predictions based on your on-chain transaction history.
